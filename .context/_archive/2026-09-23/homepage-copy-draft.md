## What I do

I help owners of garment decoration, print, embroidery and workwear businesses fix the systems that are quietly costing them money.

Most owners I meet already know something's wrong. The systems don't talk to each other. Someone's retyping supplier prices by hand. Stock is a guess. Everything runs on one person who's never allowed a day off. They know it's costing them — they just don't know where to start.

So I look at the whole business, not just the software. I follow how work actually moves through it — from quote to invoice — and I tell you, in plain English, what's broken, what it's costing you, and what to fix first.

That's the penny drop. The moment you finally see what's happening in your business, why, and what to do about it.

I've spent 25 years inside these businesses — picking, packing, despatching, running operations, implementing the ERP. Not advising from the outside. So I can usually second-guess the problem before you've finished describing it.

## The quiet cost of doing nothing

These are the numbers I see in businesses like yours most weeks. Not worst case. Just the ordinary cost of systems that don't talk to each other.

- **3–6 hours a week** lost to manual re-keying between systems.
- **2–4 hours a week** on manual invoicing and reconciliation.
- **3–5%** of inbound stock at risk where inventory is disconnected.
- **1–3%** of orders affected by picking and despatch errors.

None of it shows up as a single big bill. It shows up as margin you never see.

Four steps. Each stands on its own, and each is a natural next step from the one before it.

I follow six areas of your business from start to finish and, inside five working days, hand you a written plan that tells you what's broken, what it's costing, which software actually fits how you work, and what to fix first. The plan is yours to act on — with me or without me. Almost everyone starts here.

### Transform — rebuild how the business runs

ERP, eCommerce and process redesign run together as one programme. Scoped only once an audit has shown what it needs to cover.

### Retained — a technology director, part time

Monthly strategic and operational leadership. Roadmap ownership, vendor management, and a direct line to me.

I didn't set out to build software.
