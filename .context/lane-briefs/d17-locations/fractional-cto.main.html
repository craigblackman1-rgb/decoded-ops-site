Where the cost hides

The problems that tend to appear

These are the patterns I see most often in fractional cto engagements. They look different in every business, but the underlying structure is almost always the same.

01

No one owns the technology decisions

Vendor choices and system changes get made without a plan. Every short-term fix creates a bigger problem later, and the cost quietly grows until it shows up.

02

Growing faster than your infrastructure

Revenue is growing, but your systems are straining. Manual workarounds that worked when you were smaller now create daily friction, and your team quietly absorbs the extra work.

03

Translating between your business and your technology

You shouldn't have to become technical to get the right outcomes from your systems. Without someone who bridges both worlds, things get lost between what you asked for and what got built.

04

Vendor decisions made without independent advice

Software salespeople are good at their job. Without someone on your side who knows what you actually need, you end up with tools that solve the vendor's problem, not yours.
